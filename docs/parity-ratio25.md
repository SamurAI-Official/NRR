`same-frames` is the column that decides what a row may be used for. **yes** means the arm
was driven from this scene's own frames and scored against this scene's own targets, so its
row is a quality comparison. **no** means the arm ran in its own runtime on its own content,
so its row is a capability and latency measurement and its quality columns are empty by
construction. No sentence about quality may span a mixed pair.
